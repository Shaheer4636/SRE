1)	Discovery
	Obtain and review Discovery presentation before the Discovery meeting
	Attend the Kickoff meeting
-	Meet team members, identify peers, their location and possible common work hours
	Attend the Discovery meeting:
-	Identify target environments (GCP, AWS, Azure)
-	Review current environments to replicate like for like (e.g., Mainframe ODBC/JDBC Drivers)
-	Identify stakeholders and points of contact
-	Identify requests\requirements and possible issues of contingency
-	Verify security policies and PII data
-	Create architecture diagrams

-	Storage adjustments
